CAR MARKET TRENDS ANALYSIS

Analyzed vehicle pricing trends, mileage distributions, and fuel-type patterns using correlation heatmaps and regression visualizations.

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

The Car Market Trends Analysis is an applied automotive data science study examining secondary automotive valuation dynamics, historical price shifts, depreciation curves, and mileage sensitivities. The investigation leverages statistical regression models and multi-variable correlation heatmaps to model vehicle price retention.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Vehicle depreciation is non-linear and governed by complex compounding factors: brand equity, vehicle age, accumulated odometer mileage, transmission type, and fuel source. Both consumers and dealership operators often rely on rough heuristics rather than empirical regression models, resulting in suboptimal valuation pricing.

3. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

  • Automotive Dataset Preprocessing Clean and standardize secondary automotive sales logs, parsing numerical odometer readings, vehicle manufacture years, and categorical engine specifications.
  • Multi-Variable Correlation Heatmaps Map the statistical correlation coefficients between price, vehicle age, engine displacement, mileage, and transmission configurations.
  • Non-Linear Regression Modeling Fit regression curves depicting vehicle depreciation over 1-to-10 year lifespans across diverse manufacturer tiers.

4. KEY FEATURES

  • Luxury vs Economy Depreciation Comparison Revealed that luxury marques experience steeper early depreciation (losing ~45% in years 1-3) compared to economy counterparts (~28%).
  • Powertrain Resale Dynamics Quantified the emerging valuation premium for hybrid and fuel-efficient powertrains relative to pure diesel equivalents.
  • Mileage Elasticity Modeling Mapped non-linear price drops occurring at standard psychological odometer thresholds (e.g., 50,000 km and 100,000 km).

5. IMPLEMENTATION DETAILS

The project was implemented in Python using Pandas for feature wrangling, handling missing vehicle specs, and encoding categorical variables such as fuel type and transmission. Matplotlib and Seaborn were utilized to render correlation matrices, joint scatter-density plots, and polynomial regression trendlines.

6. RESULTS & LEARNINGS

The analysis yielded an empirical understanding of automotive market mechanics. Key learnings included recognizing non-linear relationships in tabular regression data, identifying data leakage hazards when evaluating derived vehicle age metrics, and creating polished visualizations that clearly communicate statistical trends.
